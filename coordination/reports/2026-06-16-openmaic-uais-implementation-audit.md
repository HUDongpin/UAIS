## Objective Snapshot

The active owner objective is to adopt the OpenMAIC multi-agent framework into the UAIS website at enterprise quality. Text reasoning uses DeepSeek. Multimodal, image, voice, and PPT narration use Alibaba Qwen/Model Studio. TTS must clone a 10-second teacher voice for PPT narration.
