## Assumptions

- Teachers do not auto-enter group rooms. A teacher reaches observation only through an explicit `?groupId=` deep link (which Phase 4 generates); otherwise they stay in the legacy per-course room. Students auto-enter their only group and get the picker at two or more.
- "Groups are enabled" is not directly observable from the client, so the no-group notice shows when the caller holds at least one group somewhere but none in the resolved room, or when a deep-linked groupId is not one of theirs. With no groups anywhere the room stays quiet and legacy.
- The learning-record cohort now prefers `groupId` over `classId` in a group room (`courseId/chatrooms/<cohortId>`); legacy rooms keep the previous `classId ?? "research-method-group"` behaviour, which the pinned assertion in `tests/learning-page-events.test.tsx` still covers.
- Export/Share stay wired to the `src/lib/chat-actions.ts` mocks. `createShareLink` now receives the real `groupId` when there is one instead of the hard-coded slug.

## Risks

- Mention chips break whole-message `getByText` queries for bubbles containing an `@handle`. Plain runs are deliberately left as raw text nodes so no-mention messages still match exactly, but future tests asserting a mention-bearing message must read the bubble's `textContent` (helpers exist in both live suites).
- Polling adds one GET per room per 5s per visible tab. Two tabs on one account share the 30/min actor budget; the back-off keeps that survivable but the room reads less often while throttled.
- A GET 403 now halts polling for that room and renders a notice, where the old code silently degraded to "no stored history". This is the intended graceful handling but is a behaviour change for legacy rooms too.
